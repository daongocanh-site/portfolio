About me

I design to
solves real problems for real people.

I'm Ngoc Anh, a UX/UI Designer based in France. I have worked with organisations — from start-up to established public institution — and bring a strategic and cross-functional vision to the design of digital experiences.

My approach
01
Clarity over complexity
The best solutions are often the simplest. I focus on what matters, turning it properly functional.
02
Strategy before aesthetics
Beautiful design without purpose is decoration. Every visual decision is grounded in strategic works from discovery to hypothesis validation.
03
Long-term thinking
I create systems designed to evolve. Products built on strong foundations adapt and grow with their users.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is my "personal touch" in a team ?+
The quiet force. Introverted and a Cancer at heart, I am the one who observes in silence to better understand. My strength lies in this blend of gentleness and tenacity: I don't seek to take up all the space, but I never let go of anything. With genuine kindness and an ambition that makes no compromises on quality, I make sure the project always moves toward a solution that makes sense.
What is my "recipe" for a successful product ?+
A good dose of listening, a pinch of data, and a solid understanding of business challenges. Designing means knowing how to innovate within constraints. I rely on close, iterative collaboration between design and tech to nurture an ongoing conversation with the user and turn ambitious visions into real solutions.
What makes me passionate about design ?+
It's the moment when complexity becomes clarity. Whether through a well-chosen word (UX Writing) or an intuitive interface, I love creating that feeling of 'ease' for the user. My attention to detail hides in the things no one sees, but everyone feels.
Do you know how to code ?+
I have a solid understanding of HTML/CSS and JavaScript. This helps me communicate more effectively with developpers and design things that are actually feasible to build.
Where can you work ?+
I'm open to relocate in France if it's necessary. The question depends on who I work with and what missions I'm responsible for.
